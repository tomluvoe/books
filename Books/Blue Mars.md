# Blue Mars

**Author:** [[Kim Stanley Robinson]]  
**Finished:** 2006-05-28  
**Domains:** [[Science Fiction MOC|Science Fiction]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Red Mars, the first installment in the series, starts off in the year 2026 as the first hundred humans travel to the red planet. The mixed crew of americans and russians establish a settlement called Underhill and starts the work of terraforming the planet. Pressures from Earth, personal and ideological conflicts on Mars and multiple factions make the situation, to say the least, difficult, as Mars is flooded by newcomers sent from Earth. Green Mars and Blue Mars follow Red Mars chronologically.

The trilogy books are among the most absorbing and entertaining that I have read. The series is, albeit fairly long at almost 2000 pages, very captivating and I missed it after I had finished Blue Mars. The books investigate the technological, cultural and social issues that arise during space travel and settlement and how those influences human coping and relationships. The books follow different protagonists in different chapters, each time from their personal point of view. From what first hand experience I have of technology, science and mixed cultural relationships the books are very accurate.  

Excellent!
