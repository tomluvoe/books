# End Times

**Author:** [[Peter Turchin]]  
**Finished:** 2026-10-12  
**Domains:** [[History MOC|History]], [[Politics & Society MOC|Politics & Society]]

## My review

An eye-opener. Turchin's cliodynamics treats history as something with measurable structure: elite overproduction (too many aspirants for too few positions of power), popular immiseration, a "wealth pump" moving income upward, and a state that loses fiscal and political capacity. When those line up, societies enter an age of discord — and he has the historical cases to show how often it ends badly.

What makes it land is the timing. Published in 2023, it already reads like a description of the American politics that followed: the counter-elites, the breakdown of the old consensus, the Trump-era turbulence. A model that predicts the present this well deserves to be taken seriously, even where you might argue with the details.
