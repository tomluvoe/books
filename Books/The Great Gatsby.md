# The Great Gatsby

**Author:** [[F. Scott Fitzgerald]]  
**Finished:** 2006-01-28, 2012-07-19  
**Domains:** [[Classics MOC|Classics]], [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

I re-read The Great Gatsby only because I saw a trailer of the upcoming movie this year. It's considered to be one of the best novels of the 20th century and I think the book is just great. As always, I picked up a lot of new details on the second read and it grew on me. So, I think that is it. Wonderful atmosphere, interesting characters and I love the style in which the book is written - straight forward and down to the point.
