# Get me out of here

**Author:** [[Rachel Reiland]]  
**Finished:** 2012-06-25  
**Domains:** [[Mental Health MOC|Mental Health]], [[Biography & Memoir MOC|Biography & Memoir]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Rachel (not her real name) suffers from bi-polar disorder. Anyone somewhat familiar with psychological disorders know what kind of problems that can cause a person and relationships. She is married with two children and live with her family in a house in Boston(?). In the book Rachel describes her way from the outbreak of her illness, through her several year long therapy and towards a controlled emotional life, maybe it can be called a cure. We follow the investigations into the cause of her being bi-polar and her family's dynamics and her relationships to her siblings and parents. We delve through her childhood through dialogs from her psychology sessions and slowly realize what some of the causes might be. Was her mother strong or weak in her relationship with her father? What was the power balance in the family? Was it OK to be weak? Was is OK to be a child at all? What did that mean in the long run? Very, very interesting questions. And Rachel is a good writer - I enjoyed reading every bit of the book and it pushed me just enough to actually start my university studies in psychology..
