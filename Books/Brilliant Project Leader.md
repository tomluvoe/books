# Brilliant Project Leader

**Author:** [[Mike Clayton]]  
**Finished:** 2011-12-20  
**Domains:** [[Management & Leadership MOC|Management & Leadership]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

This is actually a good book. It's not original, it is rather a summary of project management and team leading strategies. How to plan a project? How much should you involve your team/project members? How to act in crises? How to celebrate.

I have myself run projects for a few years and I have an idea of how to act under different circumstances. But this book gave me a few new ideas, and reinforced other behaviors that I already do.

Good book. I recommend it, and I think I will pick it up for a second read in a year of two.
