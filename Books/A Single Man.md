# A Single Man

**Author:** [[Christopher Isherwood]]  
**Finished:** 2011-01-16  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

This is a wonderful little book. It is about George, a professor in the 1960's California, in the midst of the Cold War. George recently lost his lover Jim and has ended up living alone and lonely in their large house.

It reminds me of books by Hemingway, although it is very different. The focus is on the human aspects of the story. Of George. His relationships with his students, neighbours and old friends.

There are many people he meets, but even if he is among people, he is lonely without Jim. But just as George is lonely is troubled, everyone else also has problems in life. Problems he helps people to manage. He has a great heart.

It is a great story. I like George. I would like to read the book again.
