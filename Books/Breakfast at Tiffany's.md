# Breakfast at Tiffany's

**Author:** [[Truman Capote]]  
**Finished:** 2012-05-16  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]], [[Classics MOC|Classics]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

It's one of those books .. yes. I love the first 2/3s of Breakfast at Tiffany's. The characters, their relationships, dialogs, Holly especially. I just love it. But then, call me stupid if you like, the book ends (perfectly fine) but has two additional story lines that leaves the city we want to be in and the characters we love to go into two additional love story plots. Do get me right, there is nothing wrong with those stories, they are good. But not at that point in time and not when I have just fallen for Holly. Then I do not want to be thrown into a different plot with different characters. I hope it is just my copy of the book that has these additional plots and not every book. I really do.
