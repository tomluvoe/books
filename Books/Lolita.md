# Lolita

**Author:** [[Vladimir Nabokov]]  
**Finished:** 2006-02-19  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]], [[Classics MOC|Classics]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Humbert Humbert, a scholar of French literature leaves France for America after his marrige has failed. He arrives in New England and rents a small room. He choose the small room when his passion was lit by seeing the house owner's young daugther sunbathing in the garden. The passion had once before been lit in his youth at a time when he had just lost his sweetheart. He becomes sexually obsessed with the twelve-year-old girl, whom he calls Lolita, and does everything in his power to please and to seduce her.

The novel is infamous and has been harshly critizised for its very controversial topic. A multitude of interpretations can be made about the novel. Taken at face value it tells the story of a tortured Humbert being manipulated and controlled by the very young and promiscuous Lolita. Another stance is to consider the story unfold through the eye's of Humbert and his interpretation of every event. In that case the story plays out as his own fantasy with Lolita as an abused member of the cast.

The story is in one sense a bit shocking in nature as humor is mixed with the debauchment of a very young girl. But today we are used to most kinds of shocking details. It is interesting, I enjoyed it a lot and Nabokov writes like a master.
