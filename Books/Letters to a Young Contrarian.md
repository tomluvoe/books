## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Letters to a Young Contrarian is a book by author and critic Christopher Hitchens. Hitchens rose to wide international fame after the publication of god is not Great and his consistent critizism of Jerry Falwell before, as well as after, Falwell's death. He has been an international correspondent in Cyprus, Chad, Uganda, Sudan, Iraq, Iran, North Korea and many other countries.

In Letters to a Young Contrarian, Hitchens mentors the reader in the art of forming an opinion, if necessary one contrary to the public's. More to the point, the book is a very well written lecture in political history and liberty, with emphasis on why opposition is important, mixed with personal anecdotes from the author.

If only more people could be like Hitchens, i.e. read this book. Arguments are nothing to fear, but useful to find common ground. I liked this book a lot. It is a quick read.
