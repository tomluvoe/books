## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Wow, I read too few books nowadays. There is something about springtime that just makes me read fewer books. Maybe the weather or something. 

Anyway, odd decision perhaps to read This is Lean if I have a limited amount of time to read books - not my first choice - but it's for work. 

Summary, it's good. Easy to read. Takes roughly 3 hours to read all the way through. It is a simple introduction to lean thinking, flow vs resource optimization. Is something I could have written a master's thesis about (although I chose to write about competence and layoffs) and is on the same level and as detailed as a master's thesis. So lean basics explained in simple terms.
