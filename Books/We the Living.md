# We the Living

**Author:** [[Ayn Rand]]  
**Finished:** 2007-06-12  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

We the living is set in post-revolution Russia between the years 1922 and 1925. The White Army has been defeated and Kira Argounova returns with her parents and sister to Petrograd. Her family, previous owners of a textile factory, find it having been nationalized and then closed. They are without a home but are able to find room with relatives. Surviving is hard, but her parents live on the hope that the NEP will allow them to once again own a business.

Kira befriends the G.P.U. agent Andrei, who fully embraces collectivism and self-sacrifice. She also initiates a relationship with Leo, an individualist who lives by and for his own values. The story revolves around the three as they struggle for existence under the evil of totalitarian rule, each with their own way of how to deal with the hardships and with their own visions and hopes for the future.

I really liked the book. It is entertaining, at the same time unsettling, what life is like under tolitarial rule. 

The background for the novel is best described by the author herself

> "I was born in Russia, I was educated under the Soviets, I have seen the conditions of existence that I describe. ... The specific events of Kira's life were not mine; her ideas, her convictions, her values were and are."
