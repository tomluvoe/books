## My review *(imported from calendar)*

This is a great management book. Full of hands-on suggestions and tips from Jack, on how to succeed in business. 

It can be read by anybody in any job, but if you have a choice, I would say to read it few weeks before you start working in your first leadership or management position. That is definately the most optimal time. It is filled with anectodes and no-bullshit advice that can then be applied and tested in the near future. And no doubt they work.
