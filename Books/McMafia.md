# McMafia

**Author:** [[Misha Glenny]]  
**Finished:** 2011-01-09  
**Domains:** [[Geopolitics MOC|Geopolitics]], [[Crime MOC|Crime]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

A serious look at the organised crime in the world. From South America, Africa, Europe, Russia, Japan and China. I was first struck by what I thought to be a silly title - McMafia. I would have to make an effort to find a worse title. But do not judge a book by its cover.

Misha has an entertaining writing style and the book is mostly based around particular crimes or crime families in each region. Describing their work or the officiers working to bring them down. Inbetween are general information about the crime situation in the region and the political decisions that have created this perfect environment for international crime to prosper.

I would recommend the book to any and all people I know. I think it is filled with information that is necessary to know in order to have a decent discussion about international affairs or the environment in which we live in.
