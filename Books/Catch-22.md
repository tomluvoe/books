# Catch-22

**Author:** [[Joseph Heller]]  
**Finished:** 2007-03-11  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]], [[Classics MOC|Classics]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Catch-22 is set in the latter stages of World War II. The main story follows Yossarian, a captain in the Air Force stationed on the island Pianosa west of Italy. Even though the main story revolves around Yossarian, each chapter follows a different person among the airmen and gives one piece at a time of the story.

The general theme in the book is its critique of bureaucratic systems and of blind obedience to rules and orders. Most of the airmen long for the day when they have completed the required number of missions and are sent home, but this is always kept from their reach due to ever increasing minimum number of missions. 

There is one other way out next to death; insanity. But herein lies the catch; If one is able to prove that one is insane, one will be sent home, but anyone who tries to prove that one is, shows a fear of death, which by default qualifies one as sane.

I liked the book a lot. I have heard that it usually tops people's list of "books I started reading but never finished", but pushing through the whole book is worth while. I laughed out loud in the last few chapters. Catch-22 is a pleasurable mixture of comic, tragic and a very dark humour. I found myself laughing at things that in a different framing would trigger a very different response in me. Worth while and an interesting read!
