# Elegance of the Hedgehog

**Author:** [[Muriel Barbery]]  
**Finished:** 2011-09-04  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Popular book? No doubt. Is it any good? Well, yes.

A girl wants to kill herself. The concierge of the building acts uneducated but secretly reads everything she can get her hands on, and enjoys arts in paintings and movies. A connoisseur. But she is treated like an uneducated person as that is the way she acts. A new man buys an apartment in the building and redecorates. He is obscure, secretive but the concierge sees through the fog. He is also a connoisseur.

A good book, but naturally tainted by what makes it popular among the masses. The heroine is by all characters in the book thought to be a nobody. As I am. But she is secretly the heroine of the universe. Therefore I like the book as it fits into my world view where I am the hero. QED.
