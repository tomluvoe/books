# Stranger in a Strange Land

**Author:** [[Robert A. Heinlein]]  
**Finished:** 2007-04-16  
**Domains:** [[Science Fiction MOC|Science Fiction]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

The stranger is Michael, a human, the man from Mars. The strange land is earth. Valentine Michael Smith was born on Mars and without human contact he was raised on the planet by aliens. But now he has been brought back to earth to meet mankind.

Being a good natured extraordinary person, he is in stark contrast to the humans he meet on earth. Greedy and power hungry, the humans seek to use him for their own benefit.

Stranger in a Strange Land could be considered Heinlein's magnum opus and the cover boasts that it is the most famous science fiction novel ever written. It has become something of a cult classic that was considered too shocking at the time of publication because the expression of individual liberty, self-responsibility and, primarily, sexual freedom. It's an exellent read and as I side with Robert concerning liberty, resposibility and freedom - I loved the message. And I loved the book!
