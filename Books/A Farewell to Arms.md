# A Farewell to Arms

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

A Farewell to Arms is a semi-autobiographical novel - Hemingway joined the Red Cross Ambulance Corps in World War I and served as an ambulance driver on the Italian Front. On his first day he assisted in collecting human remains after an ammunition factory explosion. He was later injured and commissioned to an Italian hospital where he met and fell in love with one of the nurses. Much like the novel's protagonist. That is the starting point of the book which explores the relationship between two lovers in the middle of a war.

I loved it!
