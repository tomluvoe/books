# The Sound and the Fury

**Author:** [[William Faulkner]]  
**Finished:** 2007-05-18  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]], [[Classics MOC|Classics]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

An average novel divided into four parts, three set in 1928 and one in 1910. It revolves around the Compson family in the American south. The first part is told from the eyes of a retarded family member with no sense of chronology. The story jumps back and forth in time as the protagonist is reminded of different events from his 33 year life.

The other parts are seen through the eyes of the other family members. The full picture of this dysfunctional family is not clear until the complete book has been read. Many events are not described in text, only suggested by the different characters.

Although I did not thoroughly enjoy the novel, I found it in some respects quite interesting. Mr Faulkner writes in an entertaining way and part two is engaging as the pieces from part one can be put into a larger context. But I lost interest toward part four. The foreword to the anniversary edition actually suggest that second and third reading a better than the first - but doubt I will ever read it again.

William Faulkner received the Nobel Prize for literature, in part thanks to The Sound and the Fury.
