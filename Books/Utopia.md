# Utopia

**Author:** [[Sir Thomas More]]  
**Finished:** 2006-06-05  
**Domains:** [[Philosophy MOC|Philosophy]], [[Classics MOC|Classics]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Utopia is the most well known work of Sir Thomas More. It is a recollection of a meeting between Thomas More, Peter Giles and Raphael Hythloday, where the latter describes his visit and stay in the (imaginary) nation of Utopia. It is an image of the perfect society.

But in fact Utopia is a totalitarian nation without any hint of personal freedom. Private property does not exist, and order and disciplin are of the highest importance to the commonwealth. The book describes it as the perfect society where all utopians are as happy as could possibly be, and all are without need or wish for anything else...

The book is pure propaganda, and laughable such. I found the arguments to be on a high-school paper level, and the gaping holes in the thesis are left undefended. The happy society of Utopia would without question drive any thinking person mad and towards suicide within weeks.

Saint Thomas More is the patron saint of lawyers and statesmen in the Catholic Church.
