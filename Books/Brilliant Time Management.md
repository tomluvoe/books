# Brilliant Time Management

**Author:** [[Mike Clayton]]  
**Finished:** 2011-02-19  
**Domains:** [[Management & Leadership MOC|Management & Leadership]], [[Self-Development MOC|Self-Development]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

I recently read a few books about business and personal development. Those books focus on getting ahead and to take your next career step and suggest completely different strategies than this book.

Brilliant Time Management is about doing your job effectively. Not to waste time, but to prioritize effectively. How to effectively delegate. What a good task looks like. How to finish tasks. And so on.

It is a good read. But for me, a bit .. square. A tad boring actually. The most interesting part was the chapter on saying no to tasks well and how to delegate effectively, both are difficult tasks and actually takes a lot of practice to get right. The book is worth the read for those chapters, but the rest is very basic information and mostly useful if you have a problem to finish tasks on time of feel overwhelmed by work.
