# Project Hail Mary

**Author:** [[Andy Weir]]  
**Finished:** 2026-09-06  
**Domains:** [[Science Fiction MOC|Science Fiction]]

## My review

A disappointment, especially coming back to Weir after [[The Martian]]. The central problem is the characters: everyone is superficial and stereotypical. Stratt is a one-note authority figure — put her next to the people running Mars in [[Red Mars]] and the gap is embarrassing. The crew and agency figures are cardboard too.

The book also keeps breaking its own premise. In a story where the stakes are literally the end of the world, characters pause for contemporary talking points that no one in that situation would plausibly raise — it reads as the author signalling to the reader rather than people behaving under pressure. Every time it happens the tension, such as it is, drains out. Compared to the Mars trilogy, where the politics *are* the plot and earn their place, here the messaging is bolted on.

No real excitement, no real people. The problem-solving scaffold from *The Martian* is still there, but it can't carry a novel on its own.
