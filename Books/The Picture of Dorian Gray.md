# The Picture of Dorian Gray

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

A novel that wallows in decadence and is in this day and age considered a true classic. And it is! It is a wonderful and captivating novel that investigates the dark side of the human soul. 

The Picture of Dorian Gray is Oscar Wilde's only novel. The tale is about a very beautiful young man named Dorian who wishes nothing but to keep his youth and good looks. That wish turns a painting of Dorian into a window to his soul and displays his age and agony from his many sins while he lives on forever young. In the dandy society where Dorian lives, the only measure in life is beauty, and the opportunity to sin and live life to the fullest without regret or personal agony slowly drives Dorian to develop his corrupt and morally questionable sides.
