# Critical Chain

**Author:** [[Eliyahu M. Goldratt]]  
**Finished:** 2010-07-15  
**Domains:** [[Management & Leadership MOC|Management & Leadership]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Critical Chain is definately one of the airport bookstore mangement books that a project manager shall buy on his or her way back to the office from a business trip, and completely turn over the project on arrival. Anyway, that is the wish of the all the authors, and probably the secret wish of the manager upon purchase. 

The book is a quick read, a longer flight and you'll be through the book. But is it worth it...? I think with most management books, they're not worth the time. I read some at university as part of a course where one of the tasks were to review this type of books. 

Now I am older, more experienced with business and projects, and I kind of liked this book. I do think I learnt something from the Critical Chain. It's fairly trivial, it makes sense what Goldratt writes, it should be obvious, but sometimes it is helpful to point out the trivialities. I can without hestitation say that it was good for me to read the book. The next time I plan a project or part of a project (happens a few times a year), I will apply some of the lessons from this book. I hope it will improve my output and I am certain it will. 

I will recommend people working with projects and project planning to read the book. Not necessarily of the purpose to change everything, but to reiterate and point out some valuable lessons.
