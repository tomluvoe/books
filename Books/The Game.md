## My review *(imported from calendar)*

This is an interesting book. The Game is a story about the pick up community around Mystic and Style and their project Hollywood. But it's more than just the story - the interesting part is that the methods and strategies are really about initiating conversation with people you do not know, and making them like you. That generalizes well beyond the book's setting.

So, is it worth reading? Yes, if you interact with people, this is a good book!
