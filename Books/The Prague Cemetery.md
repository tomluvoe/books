# The Prague Cemetery

**Author:** [[Umberto Eco]]  
**Finished:** 2012-08-05  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

First book by Umberto Eco that I read. I have watched most of The name of the rose movie, but that's about it. So I opened the first page of this book without knowing what to expect. Somehow I wanted to like it - but liking can not be forced. (I give Joyce a second try just about now, the first time I did not make it through the book..)

There is one particular thing that I especially like about this book, beside that it's well written, has a good pace, yada-yada. It is that all characters - except the main character - were real. And all those characters did, as far as is known by their historical output, what they do in this book. So the main character, is really a spider in the net, grease if you wish - moving history forward. Was there a man like him? Is it possible? Would history have played out this way without a person orchestrating history? All those questions are just wonderful! It triggers thinking and it builds the whole book. Great! Standing ovations to Mr Eco!
