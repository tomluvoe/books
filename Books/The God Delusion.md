# The God Delusion

**Author:** [[Richard Dawkins]]  
**Finished:** 2007-05-13  
**Domains:** [[Science & Skepticism MOC|Science & Skepticism]], [[Philosophy MOC|Philosophy]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Professor Dawkins of the University of Oxford has treaded onto religion. But what has a Zoologist to do with religion? Or any scientist for that matter? Well, science and religion come into contact because they are overlapping; religion makes certain statements about the world and science is concerned about understanding and describing the world. I know some people would disagree, but I hold that as irrefutable.

The purpose of the book is clearly stated in the introduction; it is written to inform people who have not yet thought deeply about religion that it is possible and logical to take a secular stance in the world. The book's purpose is to show people, with the use of logic and science, that there is no need to believe in an deity to understand the world. The book goes about the task by using the tools of logic, reason and science to address different teological arguments and show that they do not hold up to that type of scrutiny.

I found the book very interesting. It was a pleasure to read. It is a fairly quick and entertaining read that is very well written - as expected from Dawkins. The arguments are scientifically sound and to no real surprise religion does not hold up well to logic.
