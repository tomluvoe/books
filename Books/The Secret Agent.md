# The Secret Agent

**Author:** [[Joseph Conrad]]  
**Finished:** 2008-03-10  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]], [[Classics MOC|Classics]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Although the story holds a very powerful climax, I never became fully absorbed by the narrative and was on the whole a bit disappointed when I had finished the novel.

It is Conrad's most famous novel, published in 1907, deals with terrorism and agents provocateurs. A foreign state enlists the secret agent to provoke a powerful response from the british government and force it to suppress terrorists and limit political freedom.

It has been said that Theodore Kaczynski was inspired by The Secret Agent.
