# The Moon is a Harsh Mistress

**Author:** [[Robert A. Heinlein]]  
**Finished:** 2007-05-01  
**Domains:** [[Science Fiction MOC|Science Fiction]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

I am on small a Heinlein marathon. Well, second Heinlein in a row - and I am not disappointed.

A revolt is in the making at a lunar penal colony in the year 2075. The penal colony not a prison, it is a complete society that is most unregulated and something akin to a libertarian utopia.

The technician Mannie discovers during general computer maintenance that the Authority's mainframe computer has become self-aware. He befriends the lonely computer, and helps it develop its human side over time. The computer, traversing any data it can obtain in order to learn, senses in that data that something is very wrong with the society in the colony and asks Mannie to join an anti-Authority meeting to gather more information. The meeting is stormed by Authority soldiers and Manny meets Wyoh and a Professor as they try to avoid the soldiers. With the computer's help, the three are able to verify the fears expressed at the meeting an by the mainframe, that the lunar society is bound to fall within a few years.

The only concevable solution to stop the fall is to overthrow the current Authority and force the necessary changes to take place. The three establishes a counter movement. 

The novel has a strong political theme (libertarian) mixed with stark irony - a mix that I like. Political novels are the best. TANSTAAFL, or There Ain't No Such Thing As A Free Lunch.
