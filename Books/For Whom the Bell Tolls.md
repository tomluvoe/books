# For Whom the Bell Tolls

**Author:** [[Ernest Hemingway]]  
**Finished:** 2010-10-03  
**Domains:** [[Fiction MOC|Fiction]], [[Classics MOC|Classics]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

It is a good war story. Robert Jordan joins a band of guerrillas behind enemy lines during the Spanish Civil War under orders from the republic. He is to replace a previous bomber who was killed in an attack on a railroad, and Robert's task is to convince the group to assist him to blow a bridge, as one part of a much larger attack from the republic on the fascists. 

He joins the group a few days before the attack shall take place. If it will. He is under strict orders to prepare and stand by on the day the attack shall take place, and if it begins, blow the bridge and move back to friendly territory. 

But a good story is never as straight forward as the basic plot. Among the guerrillas is a girl named Maria. She was held captive by the fascists until the group attacked the railroad, and found her. She is young, beautiful, but has been tortured, beaten and had her head shaven. Over the few days before the attack she and Robert's feelings for each other grow.
