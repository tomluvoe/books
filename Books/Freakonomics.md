## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Ahh, math made popular. And packaged in a very interesting format. The book dives into crime, societal phenomenons, sects and what not, and investigates the cause of the events and results. From a statistical point of view. Entertaining and enlightening. 

I can not be sure the background information and conclusions are true, this is by no means my expert area. But the results are presented in a convincing manner that makes sense. I hope it is all correct. It is a likable picture.
