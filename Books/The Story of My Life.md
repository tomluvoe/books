# The Story of My Life

**Author:** [[Giacomo Casanova]]  
**Finished:** 2007-07-28  
**Domains:** [[Biography & Memoir MOC|Biography & Memoir]], [[Classics MOC|Classics]]

## My review *(imported from calendar)*

Many of my absolute favourite books that I have read are autobiographies, and this is very high on that list.

The book is a collection of the highlights of Casanova's colorful life, selected from his twelve volumes memoir.

> I am writing to laugh at myself, and I am succeeding. I write thirteen hours a day, and they pass like thirteen minutes. What pleasure in remembering one's pleasure!

Casanova wrote his memoirs after he at a respectable age had taken up residence in the Castle of Dux, Bohemia, where he would stay until the end of his extraordinary life. He reminisce events from his birth until 1774.

Born in Venice in 1725, Casanova studied Law and received his doctorate at the age of 16. His wits, charms, good looks and sharp intellect served him well, and his love for the fair sex made him a legendary seducer. His lust for adventure took him around Europe, meeting many notable figures of his time, and during his life he served in the military, as a writer, spy, entrepreneur, director, translator and gambler. He was put in prison on a number of occasions, once escaping a longer term by breaking out from The Leads in Venice.

Personally I enjoy any biography of a person worthy of having one, and Giacomo Casanova's is no exception. His life, as noted before me, is from time to time stranger than fiction and the memoir is a wonderful and exciting read. It is easy to envy his many achivements and adventures. A must read!
